GLP-1, in plain language

GLP-1 is a hormone made in the gut. Medicines that act on this pathway mimic some of its effects, including effects on appetite and blood glucose. Effects and approved uses depend on the specific product.

“Is it only for diabetes?”

Uses vary by medicine and product. In India, some products in this treatment area have weight-management indications. Ask about the exact product and its approved use.
