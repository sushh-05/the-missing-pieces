## Clinical review

Please ask a Steer clinician to review these statements before public sharing:

- The GLP-1 plain-language explanation and linked NIDDK source.
- The India-specific diabetes/weight-management disclosure and CDSCO product information.
- The wording about possible benefits, risks, and side effects varying by product and person.
- The muscle statement, its limits, and its general (not programme-specific) evidence base.
- The STEP 1 extension explanation and its limits for individual prediction.
- Whether the disclaimers and handoff copy fit Steer’s intended use.

## Usability walkthrough

Ask 3–5 people who fit the intended audience to try the demo on their own phone. Do not collect names, phone numbers, diagnoses, or detailed health histories. With their permission, observe where they hesitate and ask afterwards:

1. What did you think the experience was about from the opening screen?
2. What did you learn about GLP-1 or medical weight-management care?
3. Could you find a way to go back after choosing a concern?
4. Did the evidence notes feel understandable and trustworthy?
5. Would you keep or use the question card? What would you change?

Record only anonymous observations and changes made. Do not claim results until sessions have actually happened.

## Manual device and interaction checklist

- [ ] `placement.html` loads the embedded playable.
- [ ] `playable.html` works at 320 × 480 and on a narrow phone screen.
- [ ] Each concern puts its related lesson first in both versions.
- [ ] “Change my concern” returns to choices; choosing a different concern resets the scene.
- [ ] Both answers work for all three lessons and show their corresponding explanations.
- [ ] Source links open and remain readable on mobile.
- [ ] Copy and save card actions work.
- [ ] Keyboard users can reach choices, disclosures, dialogs, and back controls.
- [ ] Reduced-motion preference removes the piece and card animations.
- [ ] Handoff clearly says answers are not transmitted by this prototype.
