Which part do you wish someone would explain?

GLP-1, in plain language +
gut hormone→body signals→appetite
& glucose

GLP-1 is a hormone made in the gut. Medicines that act on this pathway mimic some of its effects, including effects on appetite and blood glucose. The exact effects and approved uses depend on the specific product.

NIDDK: prescription medicines and how they work ↗
“Are these medicines only for diabetes?”

Uses vary by medicine and product. In India, some products in this treatment area have weight-management indications. Ask a clinician about the exact product, its approved use and whether it could be appropriate for you.
